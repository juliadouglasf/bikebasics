Route planning*

*for Ottawa, ON, Canada

Bike Ottawa Route Planner (website)
  • Tune settings (e.g., avoid dark alleys)
  • Export the route map
  • Con: Not designed for phones
Cycling Guide (app)
  • Shows the relative safety of routes
  • Shows bike parking options
  • App developers action feedback
  • Con: Still in development (e.g., slower loading speed)
Transit (app)
  • Integrates biking with transit: great for long trips
  • Bike-only option
  • Shows the relative safety of routes
  • Loads quickly
  • Con: Often suggests more turns than necessary
